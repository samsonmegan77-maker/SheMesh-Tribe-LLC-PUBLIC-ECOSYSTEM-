# Commercial overview (public-safe)

This page describes how free tools relate to optional paid and pilot offerings.
It does **not** contain payment credentials, pilot codes, or private customer data.

## Free (this repository)

Anyone can use the Public Ecosystem tools in the browser:

- Resilience Audit
- Offline AI Butler (local templates only)
- Evidence Locker (local notes)
- Safety Centre
- Learning Path
- Community Exchange (local-only)
- 13 Public Hubs

No account. No remote database for core tools. Progress stays on the device.

## Paid products (separate fulfilment)

Optional paid products (for example a structured **Overload Audit** pack) are sold
through a **hosted payment provider** and delivered by email or download — not by
embedding card handling or API keys in this static app.

Starting price hypothesis for Full Overload Audit: **R499** (one-off). Final live
checkout links are published on SheMesh marketing pages when the merchant account
is active.

## Community and institutional pilots

Organisations may run **time-bounded pilots** (for example 30 days free access to
public tools, extendable case-by-case). Pilot administration, codes, and
participant lists are handled **privately**. They are not stored in this public
repository.

See [COMMUNITY-PILOTS.md](COMMUNITY-PILOTS.md).

## What stays private

- Proprietary algorithms and internal systems
- Payment merchant secrets
- Valid pilot-code lists
- Customer and participant personal data

If uncertain whether something is public-safe, it is not published here.
