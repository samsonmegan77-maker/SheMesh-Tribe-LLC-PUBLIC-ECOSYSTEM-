# Community pilots (public information)

## How pilots work

1. A community or organisation agrees a **start date**, **end date**, and purpose.
2. Participants use the **free Public Ecosystem tools** (this app).
3. Optional baseline and feedback forms help improve the tools.
4. Access is **time-bounded**. Extensions are decided **case-by-case**.
5. Pilot codes and participant lists are managed **privately** — not in this repo.

## First official community pilot

**Bambanani Community Care** is designated as the first official community pilot
partner for the SheMesh Tribe LLC Public Ecosystem.

| Item | Public terms |
|------|----------------|
| Access | Free public tools |
| Initial window | 30 days from activation |
| Extension | Case-by-case, by agreement |
| Price | R0 for the pilot window |

Activation dates, coordinator contacts, and any tracking codes are issued
**outside** this public repository.

## What pilots do not include

- Proprietary SheMesh internal systems
- Automatic permanent free commercial licences
- Paid product packs (unless purchased separately)
- Guaranteed outcomes

## Interested in a pilot?

Contact SheMesh Tribe LLC through the channels published on the official SheMesh
public presence. Do not post personal data or pilot codes in public GitHub issues.
