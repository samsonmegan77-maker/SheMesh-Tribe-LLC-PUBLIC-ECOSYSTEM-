# Public-Safety Boundary

## SheMesh Tribe LLC — Public Ecosystem

This document defines what may and may not appear in the public-facing repository and application.

---

## PUBLIC (allowed)

- Mission and public positioning  
- Public product descriptions  
- Generic educational material  
- Public user journeys  
- Generic checklists and templates  
- Public documentation  
- Accessible UI  
- Public-safe examples  
- Local-first / offline-first behaviour descriptions  
- Privacy statements that accurately reflect local storage  

---

## NOT PUBLIC (forbidden)

- Proprietary algorithms  
- Protected source code of internal systems  
- Trade secrets  
- Confidential technical designs  
- Unpublished inventions  
- Private datasets  
- Customer records  
- Credentials, passwords, API keys, tokens, recovery codes, private keys  
- Private infrastructure details  
- Confidential commercial agreements  
- Confidential partner information  
- Founder-only material  
- Internal business intelligence that is not intended for public release  

---

## Explicit exclusions

Do not publish or reconstruct:

- Megan Algorithm  
- SATCE Engine  
- Recursive Graph-Logic Engine  
- INDLOVU Protocol  
- Other protected technical machinery  

---

## Rule of thumb

> **If uncertain, do not publish it.**

Public release of inventions or technical material may have intellectual-property consequences. Professional legal or IP review may be appropriate before any expansion of the public surface.
